# CHIMERA Prompt

CHIMERA this observation:

[PASTE EVENT OR CLAIM]

Return:
1. pre-observation object/state;
2. record-producing interaction;
3. accessible records;
4. relevant correlates;
5. identifiable recovery key, if demonstrated;
6. explicit recovery map, if demonstrated;
7. whether the record recursively affects later dynamics;
8. in-principle information-preservation claim;
9. operational recoverability;
10. Q/K/R/D/C/U classification;
11. missing evidence and falsification test.

Anti-circularity rule: similarity of information structure is not evidence of identity of physical mechanism. Do not infer quantum behavior from cryptographic or recursive analogy. Do not call a correlate a key without specifying a recovery map. Stop after classification.
