**Correlation-History Identification, Measurement, Encryption, and Record Audit**

CHIMERA is an EFMW Zoo classifier for observational and record-writing events.

It does **not** propose that quantum measurement, cryptographic keying, and recursive feedback are one physical mechanism. Its purpose is the opposite: keep those mechanisms distinct, classify the event, identify what information remains, and stop before analogy becomes identity.

## Core question

> Did this observation encrypt information into a still-reachable correlate, stamp a readable classical record, recursively feed the record back into the system, or disperse the information beyond practical recovery?

## Fixed operation

Given a claimed observation, measurement, logging, training, publication, evaluation, or feedback event:

1. Identify the pre-observation object or state being discussed.
2. Identify the physical or informational interaction that produced the record.
3. Locate the resulting correlations and records.
4. Ask whether a distinct correlate functions as a usable recovery key.
5. Ask whether the record re-enters the dynamics of the observed system.
6. State what is recoverable **in principle** and **operationally**.
7. Assign one or more CHIMERA tags.
8. Stop. Do not infer a unified mechanism from shared tags.

## Output tags

| Tag | Class | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| `Q` | Correlated / quantum | Information may persist in global correlations while local coherence is lost. |
| `K` | Keyed | Recovery requires another identifiable and reachable correlate/key. |
| `R` | Recursive | The record becomes input to later dynamics of the same system or environment. |
| `D` | Dispersed | Relevant information is operationally unrecoverable or no recovery map/key is demonstrated. |
| `C` | Classical record | A readable persistent record exists without requiring a quantum or cryptographic interpretation. |
| `U` | Unresolved | Evidence is insufficient to classify the event. |

Hybrid tags such as `Q+K`, `K+R`, or `C+R` are allowed. Shared tags do not establish shared physics.

## Anti-circularity rule

> **Similarity of information structure is not evidence of identity of physical mechanism.**

A claim that information “still exists somewhere” does not establish practical reversibility. CHIMERA requires the claimant to specify:

- the correlate,
- the key, if any,
- the recovery map,
- the resources required,
- and a reconstruction test.

Without those, classify the claim as formal/global information preservation rather than demonstrated operational recoverability.

## Three limbs

### Quantum limb
New entanglement or correlation with another factor; local decoherence; possible persistence of global correlations.

### Cryptographic limb
Vernam-like bookkeeping: ciphertext here, key/correlate there. Key reuse, loss, corruption, or thermalization changes recoverability. This is an **information-structure analogy unless an actual cryptographic process is present**.

### Recursive limb
The observation's record enters the next state of the same system or its environment: models trained on evaluations, markets responding to reports, media responding to metrics, audits changing future behavior.

CHIMERA refuses to fuse these limbs into one physiology.

## Habitat

Use CHIMERA on processes described as:

- measurement
- observation
- logging
- training
- publication
- evaluation
- auditing
- feedback
- state reconstruction
- decoherence/reversal claims

It is especially useful when someone claims a later operation can restore a pre-observational object.

## Zoo neighbors

- **OWL** asks what was frozen and when. CHIMERA asks what kind of lock that freeze was.
- **TORTOISE** tests anti-circularity and predictive integrity. CHIMERA asks whether observation altered or destroyed the target being tested.
- **CHAMELEON** tests context/observer dependence. CHIMERA asks whether the observer wrote a persistent key or record into that context.
- **WOLF** watches weak precursors. CHIMERA asks whether those precursors are leaked keys, causal traces, or merely symptoms.
- **MAGPIE / ELEPHANT** track grounding and continuity. CHIMERA distinguishes continuity of the system from continuity of its records.

## Minimal invocation

```text
CHIMERA this observation.
```

Expanded:

```text
Classify this event with CHIMERA. Identify the pre-observation state, interaction,
records/correlates, possible key, recovery map, recursive feedback, operational
recoverability, and final Q/K/R/D/C/U tags. Apply the anti-circularity rule and
do not infer common physical mechanisms from structural analogy.
```

## Status

CHIMERA is a methodological classifier / research discipline within the EFMW Zoo. It is **not**, by itself, evidence for a new physical law, a unified theory of observation, or equivalence among quantum mechanics, cryptography, and recursive systems.
